# Plan 3b: checks by hand

These are done by the owner in the real Slack workspace with a real Google account, after the migration is applied, the code is deployed and the two Google secrets are set. (Check 1 is the exception: do it before the secrets are set.) Each check says what to do, then "Expect:" with what must be seen.

1. Before the secrets are set, type `/ooo google` in Slack. Expect: the private answer "Google isn't set up for this Steward yet." Then type `/ooo` and save "Working remotely". Expect: it works as before, with the Slack status line only.
2. Set the two secrets, then type `/ooo google`. Expect: a private message "Connect Google and Steward will also set your calendar and Gmail auto-reply" with a "Connect Google" link, and the line "The link works for ten minutes." Open the link. Expect: a page "Connect Google to Steward" that names you and the Slack workspace, and says Steward does not read your email. Press "Continue to Google" and allow everything. Expect: the page "Google is connected". Type `/ooo google` again. Expect: "Google is connected as" and your Google address.
3. Type `/ooo`, choose "Out of office", from today to tomorrow, and save. Expect: a card titled "Out of office" with today and tomorrow, and three lines: "Slack status set", "Calendar event added" and "Gmail auto-reply on", the last two with the same dates. Your Slack status shows. Your main Google Calendar has an out-of-office event covering both days. Gmail's settings show the auto-reply on with the dates.
4. Press Undo on that card within five minutes. Expect: the card is titled "Undone", with the lines "Slack status cleared", "Calendar event removed" and "Gmail auto-reply turned off". The Slack status is gone, the calendar event is gone, and the Gmail auto-reply is off.
5. Type `/ooo`, choose "Out of office", with a first day next week, and save. Expect: the card is titled "Scheduled" and shows "Calendar event added" and "Gmail auto-reply on" at once, and for Slack "Slack status, will be set when it starts" with the first day. The calendar event and the auto-reply are already there, and your Slack status is not set. On the first day, within a minute or two of midnight in your time zone, the Slack status appears. Expect: still only one event on the calendar, not two.
6. Before the first day, press Cancel on the scheduled card (or save another one for next week and cancel that). The code does take the Google writes back when a scheduled status is cancelled. Expect: the card is titled "Cancelled", with the lines "Slack status, won't be set", "Calendar event removed" and "Gmail auto-reply turned off". The event is gone from the calendar and the auto-reply is off.
7. In Gmail's settings turn on an auto-reply by hand, with your own subject and text. Then save "Out of office" in Steward. Expect: the card has a line "Gmail auto-reply skipped" that says your Gmail auto-reply is already on and Steward left it alone. Your own text in Gmail is unchanged. The calendar event is still added. Turn your own auto-reply off again when you are done.
8. Gmail's own rounding. Do check 3 again. Open Gmail's settings and read the auto-reply. Expect: the subject is "Out of office" and the body is "I'm out of office from" the first day "to" the last day "and will reply when I'm back." (On a one-day status: "I'm out of office on" the day, then the same ending.) Write down anything Gmail changed in the subject or the body. Then press Undo. Expect: the auto-reply is turned off. Steward recognises its own auto-reply by subject and body, ignoring spaces and line breaks at the two ends and ignoring the times. If Gmail changed the words in any other way, Undo will leave the auto-reply on, and that is a problem to report.
9. Save "Remote" ("Working remotely"). Expect: the Slack status only. There is no calendar event, no Gmail auto-reply and no hint to connect Google.
10. Ask a colleague who has not connected Google to save "Out of office" (or disconnect yourself first, see check 11). Expect: the card has a hint line: "Connect Google and Steward will also set your calendar and Gmail auto-reply, run /ooo google" (the card shows it with a dash before "run").
11. Type `/ooo google disconnect`. Expect: "Google is disconnected. Events and auto-replies Steward already set stay as they are." In the Google account's page for third-party access (myaccount.google.com, Security), Steward is gone. Then save "Out of office". Expect: the hint line from check 10 shows again. Type `/ooo google disconnect` once more. Expect: "Google isn't connected."
12. Connect again. After seven days in Testing, or after removing Steward's access in the Google account by hand, save "Out of office". Expect: the Slack status is set, and the calendar and Gmail lines say "skipped" with "Reconnect Google, run /ooo google". Type `/ooo google`. Expect: "Steward lost its access to Google." with a "Reconnect Google" link.

Write down the date and the result beside each check.
